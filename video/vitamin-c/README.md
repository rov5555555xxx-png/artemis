# ลุงเฉลย — วิตามินซี

Episode 2 of the uncle-and-nephew explainer series: from scurvy at sea and James Lind's 1747 trial
to Szent-Györgyi's paprika, Linus Pauling, and what the Cochrane review says about colds.

Same pipeline as `../vitamin-d3` (see its README): `./make.sh` builds narration, music, mix and
renders `vitamin-c-lungchalei.mp4`. Narration lines are `LINES` in `build_vo.py`; on-screen
subtitles are `SUBS` in `index.html`, in the same order.

Voices used: uncle `pqHfZKP75CvOlQylNhV4` (Bill), nephew `TX3LPaxmHKxFdv7VOQHJ` (Liam), model `eleven_v4`.
